EduVerse Support

EduVerse is a self-service app. Most things can be fixed in the app or in your iPhone settings, as described below. Replies to individual messages are not offered.

Common questions

  • "You've used all of today's free AI help." It refills tomorrow. A grown-up can unlock more with EduVerse Pro in Settings, then Grown-ups.
  • How do I cancel Pro? On the iPhone: Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then EduVerse.
  • I bought Pro but it says Free. Open Settings, Grown-ups, then Restore purchases. You need to be signed in with the same Apple ID.
  • How do I move courses to a new phone? Settings, Backup and transfer, Export. Paste the code into Import on the new phone.
  • How do I delete everything? Settings, Grown-ups, Delete all my data.
  • Something the AI said worried me. AI answers can be wrong, so check important things with a trusted adult or a teacher. If a child is upset or in danger, talk to a grown-up or call your local emergency number (112 in India).
