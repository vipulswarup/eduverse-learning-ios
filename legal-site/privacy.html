EduVerse Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026. Provided by Anay.

EduVerse is a learning app for ages 5 to 20. An account is optional. There are no ads and no tracking, and we collect as little as we can.

If you do not make an account

Everything (courses, lessons, XP, badges, settings, the birth month and year asked in the welcome tour) stays on the device. Nothing is sent to us.

If you make an account

Accounts are stored on our server (hosted by Cloudflare). We store:

  • a username you choose (please do not use a real name), and a scrambled (hashed) copy of the password and recovery code. We cannot read your password;
  • whether a parent or an adult made the account, and whether the learner is 13 or over (we do not store the birth date);
  • your saved progress (courses, XP, streak, badges, settings) so it can be restored on another device;
  • if you turn on two-step verification, the secret for your authenticator app and backup codes (stored hashed);
  • friend links, blocks and reports, weekly XP, league membership with the nickname you pick, challenges and quests you send or receive.

We do not collect email addresses, phone numbers, real names, photos or locations.

Children

  • Under 18: an account must be made by a parent or guardian, who agrees to this policy on the child's behalf.
  • Under 13: the account only saves progress. Friends, leagues, challenges to others and quests are switched off.
  • A parent can delete the account and all its server data at any time: Settings → Account → Delete account. This removes the account, progress, friend links and league entries.

Friends, leagues, challenges and quests (13 and over)

Friends find each other by exact username or friend code. Friends and league members can see the username or chosen nickname and weekly XP. A challenge contains questions, a score and a nickname and is only shared if the learner sends it. Learners can block or report another user from the friend menu.

Photos and voice

Text in a homework photo is read on the iPhone with Apple's Vision technology. The photo is never uploaded. Voice commands, if turned on, use Apple's speech recognition; no audio is kept.

The online AI (Groq)

When the iPhone can't answer on its own, the text of the request (a topic, a question, or text read from a photo) is sent through our relay to Groq, Inc., which runs the AI models. We do not store these requests. Learners should not type personal information; the app asks the AI never to request it.

Purchases

Subscriptions, if offered, are handled by Apple In-App Purchase. We never see payment details.

Your choices

  • Delete the account and server data: Settings → Account → Delete account.
  • Delete everything on the device: Settings → Grown-ups → Delete all my data.
  • Turn on two-step verification: Settings → Account.
  • Servers keep technical logs for a short time to prevent abuse. Data is kept only until the account is deleted.
