EduVerse Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026. Provided by Anay.

EduVerse is a learning app for ages 5 to 20. We built it to collect as little as possible. EduVerse has no server of its own and no accounts run by us. There are no ads and no tracking.

What stays on the device

  • Courses, lessons, XP, badges, settings, the birth month and year asked in the welcome tour, and the child's first name typed in the grown-up view. These are stored on the iPhone and, if iCloud is on, in the learner's own iCloud (see below).
  • Photos of homework. The text in the photo is read on the iPhone by Apple's Vision technology. The photo itself is never uploaded.
  • Voice commands, if voice control is turned on. Apple's speech recognition understands them, on the device when the language allows. No audio is kept.

Services from Apple

  • iCloud. Progress is saved in the learner's private iCloud so it appears on their other devices. Only that Apple ID can read it; we cannot. "Delete all my data" in Settings → Grown-ups removes it.
  • Game Center (13 and over). Friends, friend requests and the weekly XP leaderboard use Apple's Game Center. Game Center shows the learner's Game Center nickname and weekly XP to their Game Center friends. Apple runs it, including reporting and the parental controls in Screen Time.
  • Apple Intelligence (on supported iPhones). When available, EduVerse first asks Apple's on-device model. That happens entirely on the iPhone.
  • In-App Purchases. Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never see payment details.

The online AI (Groq)

When the iPhone can't answer on its own, the text of the request (for example a topic, a question, or the text read from a homework photo) is sent to Groq, Inc., which runs the AI models, to write the answer. Groq says it does not keep this data by default. We do not receive or store these requests. Learners should not type personal information; the app asks the AI never to request it.

Challenges and share cards

A challenge code contains the game's questions, the score and the nickname the learner chose. It is only shared if the learner sends it. Share cards are pictures made on the iPhone. Under-13s can't use challenges, share cards or friends.

Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not run accounts, we do not see learning data, and features that involve other people are limited to ages 13 and over and to Apple's Game Center.

Your choices

  • Delete everything: Settings → Grown-ups → Delete all my data.
  • Turn off Apple Intelligence use in Settings → Grown-ups, or iCloud for EduVerse in the iPhone's Settings → your name → iCloud.
  • EduVerse keeps no account or server data about you, so there is nothing for us to look up or delete on our side. Everything is on your device and in your own iCloud, and you can delete it yourself using the button above.
